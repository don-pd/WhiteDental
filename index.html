Our Services

Root Canal

Preserves and protects decayed or infected teeth from extraction using therapy.

Tooth Extraction

Painless extraction of damaged or decayed teeth for children and adults.

Pediatric Dentistry

Specialty care to preserve children's teeth and guide permanent teeth.

Orthodontics

Realignment of crooked, crowded, or protruding teeth using dental braces.

Digital X-Ray

Quick, high-precision advanced imaging for accurate diagnosis.
